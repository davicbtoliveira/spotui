# SpotUI

SpotUI is a terminal music client centered on browsing a listener's Spotify
collection and controlling local playback.

## Language

**Library**:
The listener's collection of Liked Tracks, User Playlists, and Saved Albums.
_Avoid_: Collection, My Music

**Liked Track**:
A track the listener has explicitly saved to their Library.
_Avoid_: Favorite Track, Saved Song

**User Playlist**:
A playlist that appears in the listener's Library, whether owned or followed.
_Avoid_: Personal Playlist

**Public Playlist**:
A Spotify playlist that can be discovered outside the listener's Library, including editorial and other listeners' playlists.

**Saved Album**:
An album the listener has explicitly saved to their Library.
_Avoid_: Favorite Album
